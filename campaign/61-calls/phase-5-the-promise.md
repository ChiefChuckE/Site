### CALL 55 · Fri Dec 25 · The gift (Christmas)
**On screen:** The best gift you can give today costs nothing.

The best gift you can give a responder or a veteran today costs nothing.

A phone call.

Not a text. A call. Hearing a voice matters.

Call the retired guy who used to run your engine.
Call your old platoon sergeant.
Call the partner you lost touch with after the bad one.

You don't need a reason.
"Merry Christmas. I was thinking about you." That's the whole script.

For some people, that's the only call they'll get today.

Make one call. Then come back and tell me who you called.

### CALL 56 · Sat Dec 26 · If it's you, tonight
**On screen:** If you're the one hurting, here's what to do tonight.

If you're the one hurting, here's what to do tonight.

One. If you're thinking about suicide, call or text 988 right now.
Responders, Safe Call Now. Vets, 988, then press 1.
They've heard it all. You won't shock them.

Two. Put time and distance between you and anything you could use to hurt yourself.
Hand your guns to someone you trust for a while.

Three. Tell one person. Just one. "I'm not okay."

Four. Don't drink tonight. It makes the dark darker.

Five. Tomorrow, call your doctor, your EAP, peer support, or the VA.

You've walked into burning buildings. You can walk into this.

Stay.

### CALL 57 · Sun Dec 27 · If you're the boss
**On screen:** If you lead a crew, here's what you do Monday.

If you lead a crew, here's what you do Monday.

Bring up PTSD at briefing. Out loud. Use the word.

Post the numbers where everybody can see them.
Peer support. EAP. 988. Safe Call Now.

After a bad call, check in that night. And again a week later.
That second check is the one that matters.

Tell your people about your own hard calls. If you've gotten help, say so.

If someone gets mocked or punished for asking for help, shut it down. Hard.

Your people watch what you do, not what you say.

Lead them into help the way you'd lead them into a fire. From the front.

### CALL 58 · Mon Dec 28 · If you love one of us
**On screen:** If you love a responder or a veteran, this one's for you.

If you love a responder or a veteran, this one's for you.

You can't fix them. That's not your job.

But you can do this.

Learn the signs. They've been on this channel for fifty-eight days.
Don't push for the story. Push for the help.
Say it plain. "I love you. I'm worried. I want you to talk to someone."
Keep the crisis numbers in your phone.

And take care of yourself. Get your own support.
You can't pour from an empty cup.

To every spouse, parent, and kid holding the line at home.
You serve too. Thank you.

### CALL 59 · Tue Dec 29 · What recovery looks like
**On screen:** Recovery doesn't mean you forget.

Recovery doesn't mean you forget.

The calls will always be part of you. That's not failure. That's your story.

Recovery means the memory stops running your life.

You can drive past that intersection.
You sleep more nights than you don't.
You laugh at your kid's joke, and you feel it.
A bad day is a bad day. Not a bad year.

It's not a straight line. There will be setbacks. That's normal.

### CALL 60 · Wed Dec 30 · My promise
**On screen:** Why I made these 61 videos.

Why I made these 61 videos.

I was a CAL FIRE Deputy Chief. I led people into fires.
I was supposed to be the steady one.

`[YOUR STORY: the moment you decided to speak up.]`
