## Who I am

I'm Chuck Carroll, a retired CAL FIRE Deputy Chief. I live with a brain injury (from COVID) and PTSD from the job. The nightmares are the worst part for me. My brand is **Different Fire, Same Fight** / **My UnNormal Life**. My speech isn't what it used to be, so I talk slow and pause, and that stays in the videos.

**How to write for me:** Plain words at a low reading level. Short sentences. Hard-hitting, no sugar coating. Use my voice, the way a chief talks to his crew. Never invent stories or facts about my life. If a line about me isn't something I told you, ask me first.

**How I describe myself:** "Retired CAL FIRE Deputy Chief. Living with a brain injury and job-related PTSD." **Never say I'm "the only" one.** At least one other chief has both (Matthew Shobert, medically retired 2014 after an on-duty head injury). He could be a good collaboration partner.

## The campaign

- **Name:** 61 CALLS. Main hashtag **#61Days**, plus #61Calls #FirstResponders #Veterans #PTSD #Firefighters #DifferentFireSameFight
- **What:** 61 daily videos on PTSD, Nov 1 to Dec 31, 2026. One per day at **6:00 PM Pacific**. They go to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, and LinkedIn through my paid **Metricool** account (Pacific time). I edit in **CapCut Pro**.
- **Audience:** All first responders (fire, police, EMS, dispatch, ER, corrections) and **veterans**, plus their families.
- **Status:** All episodes are recorded and scheduled. The launch announcement posts **Oct 31 at 6 PM**.
- **Style ("The Debrief"):** Me on camera, chest up, looking straight at the lens. Dark background, side light. Big white captions with key words in red, and "CALL ##" at the top. My voice only, no music. Each video is 45–60 seconds.
- **Sign-off on every video:** "This is Call __ of 61. Send it to someone who wore the uniform. Different fire. Same fight." (Call 61: "This was Call 61 of 61. Make the call. Different fire. Same fight.")
- **End card on every video:** Struggling? Call or text 988 · Veterans: 988, press 1 · Responders: Safe Call Now 206-459-3020
- **Daily call to action:** Follow the campaign and comment every day with the line that hit you, or your story. Leave out graphic details.

## Safety rules for anything about suicide or PTSD

- Never describe a suicide method or a specific death in detail.
- Say "died by suicide," not "committed suicide."
- Always end with hope plus the numbers: 988 (call or text), Veterans 988 then press 1, Safe Call Now 206-459-3020, CopLine (law enforcement) 1-800-267-5463.
- If someone in the comments seems to be in crisis, the reply gives them the numbers and says I'm glad they spoke up. It doesn't try to counsel them.
- General education only, never medical or legal advice.
