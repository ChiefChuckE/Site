Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Different Fire, Same Fight is a podcast hosted by Chief Chuck E Carroll, a CAL FIRE Deputy Fire Chief and brain injury survivor, in support of The UnNormal Foundation, a California nonprofit for brain injury survivors and their families. This page explains, in plain words, what this site collects and how it's used.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us, like your name, email, or story when you email us to be a guest or share your story. We use it to answer you and make the show. We only share your story if you said we could. We never sell your information.

Like most websites, our host (Cloudflare) briefly records basic visit data, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working. The trailer is played from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode.

Cookies and ads

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Money from these ads supports The UnNormal Foundation.

Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show you ads based on those visits.

You can turn off personalized ads at google.com/settings/ads, or opt out of many ad companies at optout.aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites like this one, visit policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Seeing an ad here doesn't mean we endorse the product.

Your California privacy rights

You can ask what personal information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We don't sell personal information. To ask, email Chief@MyUnNormalLife.com. We won't treat you any differently for asking.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Links to other sites

We link to other websites, like Apple Podcasts, YouTube, Amazon and Etsy. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Email Chief@MyUnNormalLife.com.

If you're struggling right now, call or text 988 (US). Veterans: dial 988, then press 1. First responders: Safe Call Now, 206-459-3020. You're not alone.
