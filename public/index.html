About Chief Chuck

Chuck Carroll is a CAL FIRE Deputy Fire Chief, and he will always be a firefighter. Then a brain injury changed how he thinks, remembers and speaks. He writes poetry and books for survivors under the My UnNormal Life name, and he hosts this show to sit down with others who are fighting their own fire.

His book Welcome to Your New UnNormal and printable tools for survivors are available below.
