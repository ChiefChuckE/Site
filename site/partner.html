Sponsors

How sponsoring works.

  1. Pick your lengths. 15, 30 or 45 seconds, or all three.
  2. Pick the voices. Michael and other survivors, or your own people (players, staff, first responders) saying the campaign lines.
  3. We build it. Your ads are cut in days, not weeks, and you approve every one.
  4. Post it everywhere. You get the finished files with your name on the end card, plus a spot on this site.

What you get

  • Your name on the end card: "This ad paid for by …"
  • Ready-to-post vertical videos for your own channels
  • The option to feature your own people in the ads
  • Recognition on DrinkOrDrive.org as a campaign partner
  • A real story behind your name, not a stock-photo PSA

Who it fits

  • Sports teams and their foundations
  • Bars, restaurants and venues
  • Rideshare, taxi and towing companies
  • Insurance agencies, auto dealers and law firms
  • Hospitals, schools and first responder associations

Packages are built around your budget and your audience. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll put a plan together.
