Who I am

Retired fire chief. Brain injury survivor.

I'm Chuck Carroll, a retired CAL FIRE Deputy Fire Chief. After a career spent leading people, communicating, and solving problems, COVID handed me something I didn't ask for: a brain injury that rewrote my life when I wasn't looking.

The part that got to me most was losing my words. Then they came back, just not the way I expected. They came back as poetry.

I believe my brain injury has a purpose: to connect survivors together. I used to lead firefighters into fires. Now I want to lead for brain injury survivors, because we only really understand each other.

My speech isn't what it used to be, and I share anyway. If you see me pause or stumble, that's normal. It's not cut out.
