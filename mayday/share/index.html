MAYDAY. It's the one word every firefighter is trained to say for someone else, and almost none of us ever say for ourselves.

Chief Chuck E Carroll is a career firefighter and CAL FIRE Deputy Chief who lives with PTSD from the job and a brain injury from COVID. For 61 days, Nov 1 through New Year's Eve, he's posting one short video a day at 6 PM Pacific: what nobody told him, what the numbers say, what it looks like before it's a crisis, and what works, by name, so you can ask for it. Nobody asked him to. Nobody's paying for it.
